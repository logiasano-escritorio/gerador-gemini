## BASE (vai antes de cada persona, todos os 15 prompts)

```
Hyperrealistic amateur iPhone 7 selfie photo, hand-held casual UGC style.

The person is holding the EXACT product/bottle from the reference image — reproduce it 1:1, do not redesign the label, copy each character of the packaging exactly. The product is CLOSED, vertical, label facing the camera.

CRITICAL PRODUCT SIZE: the product height should be approximately equal to the width of the person's open palm. The product must be smaller than the person's face/head. It fits naturally between thumb and pinky when gripped. NEVER oversized, NEVER bigger than the head. Think "everyday supplement bottle in hand", not "trophy".

Person is looking directly at the camera with a soft genuine smile, warm eye contact, slight micro-expression — NOT model-posed.

iPhone 7 camera quality: subtle digital noise in the shadows, mild lens flare if there is a window, realistic skin texture (visible pores, peach fuzz on cheeks, fine lines around eyes for older subjects), NO airbrushing, NO plastic AI skin, sharp focus on face and product label, mild phone-level depth of field (not DSLR bokeh).

Looks like a real customer review photo posted on Instagram Stories or WhatsApp Status, NOT a brand campaign.

NO text overlays, NO watermarks, NO captions, NO UI elements, NO ring light, NO professional studio lighting, NO multiple bottles, NO redesigned label.
```
